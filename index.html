Operational Design · Process Architecture · From Scratch

Building the Operating System of a Growing Business

Forever Spaces · Dec 2025 – Present · Business Analyst & Operations Lead

The Problem

A scaling business running on informal knowledge and no process infrastructure

Forever Spaces, a growing interiors company, had no documented processes across its core functions. Procurement was ad hoc — orders were placed without structured lead-time planning, specification checks, or a formal change management path. Project management relied on tribal knowledge, and there was no single view of business performance. The Managing Director needed the business to be scalable, but had no operational baseline to build from.

How I worked

1
DiscoveryConducted stakeholder interviews across sales, design and operations to map current workflows and identify failure points — including where missed spec checks and late supplier flags were causing rework.
2
Process design from zeroDesigned and formalised end-to-end processes for procurement, project management, sales pipeline and order management — none existed before. Each process was structured, documented and handed off to the relevant owner.
3
KPI frameworkBuilt a business scorecard tracking sales conversion, project completion rate, snag-free delivery rate and time-to-completion — giving the MD their first structured view of operational performance.
4
EmbeddingIntroduced communication cadences and handoff structures between design, sales and operations to reduce scheduling friction and improve cross-functional coordination.

What was delivered

4
Core processes designed and formalised from zero
1st
Business scorecard — company's first KPI framework
Procurement · Project management · Sales pipeline · Order management
Functional areas taken from informal to fully documented

Tools & Methods

Process mapping KPI design Stakeholder interviews Gap analysis KBBConnect CRM Requirements elicitation As-is / to-be design

Key learning

In small businesses, the BA's job is translation in both directions — turning ambiguous founder intent into structured process, and structured process back into language the team will actually follow. The scorecard only worked because every metric was defined with the people who would own it.

Process artefact — created & formalised at Forever Spaces

Procurement process flow

Designed from scratch to bring structure to an ad hoc ordering process — covering four phases: planning, verification, change management, and order placement. Includes decision logic for long lead-time items (Brass finishes, Utopia worktops, Whirlpool systems) and a formal exception path for specification changes.

Using EPOS Data to Drive Store Performance at Tesco

Tesco · Jul 2024 – Dec 2025 · Retail Business Analyst

The Problem

Dashboard data available but not consistently translated into action

Tesco's store had access to rich operational data — transaction voids, shrink boards, waste reporting, sales performance — via Power BI and Tableau. The challenge wasn't data availability; it was converting daily outputs into prioritised, actionable decisions for store management in a fast-moving retail environment.

How I worked

1
Daily MonitoringTracked Power BI and Tableau dashboards across transaction voids, shrink, waste and sales performance — identifying anomalies and trends before they became incidents.
2
80/20 AnalysisApplied Pareto analysis to identify the 20% of SKU placements and operational issues driving 80% of waste, loss and underperformance.
3
Insight PresentationPresented findings to store management on profitability, promotional performance and top-loss categories — translating data into specific layout and stock recommendations.

Outcomes

15%

Tools & Methods

Key learning

Most organisations have more data than insight. The analyst's value isn't in reading dashboards — it's in knowing which number changes first, what it signals downstream, and how to present that to a decision-maker who has 90 seconds to act on it.
